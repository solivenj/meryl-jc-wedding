    The idea

    A road that climbs to the wedding

    The reference site is one long tree that you climb from the roots. Ours is one long road that climbs a hill, and the hill is Todi. Every town in Umbria sits on a hilltop, and the roads up to them switch back and forth across the slope. So the road crosses the page and comes back, and it climbs while it does it. Each straight stretch is one part of your life, and each hairpin bend is the turn into the next.

    Scrolling down drives the car forward and the scenery slides down past it, so the reader feels the climb. The road ahead is drawn in pencil and inks itself in behind the car. At the top the car parks in the piazza, the keys go on a hook, and the closing line appears.

The odometer switches to kilometres. The hairpin at the end is the flight home.

    What makes it yours

    The details

    NJ and CA become one road

    The opening is where you both come from, merging in Boston.

    The road inks itself in

    Pencil ahead, ink behind. Scroll back up and the ink turns back to pencil.

    Long distance feels long

    Double the scrolling, five flights, a full five-bar tally.

    The odometer keeps honest time

    It stops while you fly, counts in kilometres in Italy, and goes back to miles when you come home.

    A passport fills up

    Stamps land for Italy 2022, Bali, Spain and Italy 2027. The half stops are just a pause and a stamp, so they never slow the story.

    Polaroid pickets

    Real photos as a fence along the road, overlapping like a stack on a fridge door.

    How it gets built

    Inside this repo

    No new dependencies. The site already ships motion 12 for scroll progress. The rest is one inline SVG and transforms written straight to the page on each frame, so React never re-renders while scrolling.

    app/italy/our-story/
  page.tsx            server component: the story as real text, the road on top
components/italy/road/
  RoadTrip.tsx        "use client": sticky stage, scroll → progress, one animation loop
  route.ts            this prototype's geometry and scroll weights, copied number for number
  Car.tsx ItCar.tsx Plane.tsx
  scenery/*.tsx       one file per stop, ported from this prototype's drawings unchanged
  Hud.tsx             road-sign label, odometer, stop dots
lib/italy-content.ts  ITALY_STORY.chapters gains a `stop` key: sign, pause, half stop
scripts/road-shot.mjs Playwright: the prototype and the real page at every stop, side by side
    • It will look like this page. The build reuses this prototype's coordinates, drawings, type and timings. A screenshot script captures both at every stop on a laptop and a phone, and the build isn't done until each pair matches.
    • Text stays real. Every chapter is a real heading and paragraph in the HTML, so screen readers, search and copy-paste work.
    • Fallback. With reduced motion on, or before the script loads, readers get today's alternating bands with the same copy.
    • Phones. The camera follows the car sideways as well as up and the chapter text docks at the bottom, as in this prototype.
