## attire/ — dress-code references

Processed by `scripts/process-photos.mjs` (resize to 1600px long edge, WebP,
EXIF stripped).

These are **mood references**, collected from a publicly published wedding
website (davidandlina.com) that had in turn collected them as attire
inspiration. They are not licensed to us, and the provenance of the originals
is unknown.

That is an accepted risk **only** while this site stays private: it sits
behind the password gate (`proxy.ts`) and `app/robots.ts` disallows
everything, so the pages are not indexed and not publicly reachable. If the
site is ever opened up, these need replacing with licensed stock
(Unsplash/Pexels are free for commercial use) or with photographs of our own.

Deliberately **not** taken from that site: their engagement photographs,
their venue, their car, and their "LD" wax seal — another couple's faces and
monogram have no business here regardless of licensing.
