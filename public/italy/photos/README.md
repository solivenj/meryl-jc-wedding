# Home page carousel photos

Put photos here (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp` or `.avif`). They show up in the slow-scrolling band at the top of /italy-wedding, in filename order. Name them `01-…`, `02-…` and so on to control the sequence.

- Portrait or square photos work best. Each slide is cropped to 4:5.
- Aim for about 1600px on the long edge. Larger files are fine, because next/image resizes them.
- You need at least 3 photos. With fewer, the band shows the warm placeholder panels.
